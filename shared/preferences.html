Mute Block v0.0.0
Auto Switch To Answers
Flip All Related to Answers before adding the FOP button.
FOP Button Delay (ms)
Delay before showing the Original Poster button.
Answers Only Flip Delay (ms)
Delay after switching to Answers before showing the OP button.
Auto-Start Current Work
Resume work on browser start. Leave off to wait until you navigate to Quora.
Profiles Per Batch
How many profiles to open or nuke at one time from profile lists. Space-feed nukes use an internal safety cap.
Worker Window Focus
What a queued action does to its tab in the worker window. Focus brings the tab forward and takes OS focus on each action. Activate brings it forward without taking focus. None keeps it in the background and takes no focus, so steps that need the page visible wait, then fail. Applies from the next action.
Recent History Retention (days)
Recent history older than this is pruned. Whichever limit is hit first prunes. Use `0` to disable age pruning.
Recent History Entry Cap
Keep at most this many recent history entries, trimming the oldest first. Whichever limit is hit first prunes. Use `0` to disable the cap.
Categorized Spaces
Saved space classifications can be resorted and changed here.
Erase Everything
This permanently deletes everything the extension has stored: the record of every profile blocked, the queue, the run history, the statistics, the outlier list, your preferences and your categorized spaces. The extension then starts as a fresh install, and it will act on every profile it has blocked before. Do not use this routinely. Use it only before a clean reinstall or to start over.
Pause the Queue on the Dashboard first. The erase is refused while any work is running. The extension reloads, which stops its background worker, once the erase is scheduled.
